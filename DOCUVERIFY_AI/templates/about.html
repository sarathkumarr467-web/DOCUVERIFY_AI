📄 DOCUVERIFY AI

DOCUVERIFY AI is an intelligent document verification system designed to analyze digital documents and identify possible signs of tampering, manipulation, or suspicious content. The system combines document processing, feature analysis, and machine learning to provide a simple and explainable verification result.

🎯 Our Purpose

To make document verification faster, smarter, and easier by automatically analyzing important document characteristics and detecting potential authenticity issues.

⚙️ How It Works

The document is uploaded, its content is extracted, important features are analyzed, and the trained machine learning model predicts whether the document is Genuine, Suspicious, or Fake.
